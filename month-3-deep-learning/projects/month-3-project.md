# Month 3 Project — Train a Neural Network

Build, train, and explain a neural network on a dataset you care about.

## Pick one
1. **Image classifier** — your own categories (e.g. a few object classes).
2. **Text classifier** — sentiment, topic, or spam-vs-not (no real personal data).
3. **Tiny generator** — character-level text generation in the style of a corpus.

## Requirements
- Defined as an `nn.Module` with your own architecture choices.
- A proper training loop with a train/validation split.
- Training and validation curves plotted.
- A short `README.md`: the task, your architecture, final metrics, and one paragraph
  on **what the model learned** (and where it fails).

## Stretch goals
- Add regularization (dropout) and compare curves with/without.
- Use a pre-trained model and fine-tune it (transfer learning).
- Export the model and write an inference script.

## Done when
You can explain *why* you chose your architecture and *what* the loss curves mean.
