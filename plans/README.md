# Learning Plans

All my AI learning plans in one place. They overlap on purpose, so use this to see how they fit together.

| Plan | Pace | Focus |
|------|------|-------|
| [5-month course](../README.md) | Weekly labs | Top-down: build first, then ML, deep learning, agents, math |
| [6-month power-user plan](6-month-power-user.md) | 30 min/day | Becoming the AI power user at work: mental models, agentic coding, RAG, API, evals, teaching |
| [Mac local AI stack](mac-local-stack.md) | ~1 hr sessions | Local LLMs, LiteLLM, MCP, agents, and RAG on the M4 Pro (built in [`/lab`](../lab/)) |

## How they fit

- **Course Months 1–3** have no equivalent elsewhere. Do them in order.
- **Course Month 4, power-user Months 2–4, and the Mac stack** are the same ground: agents, MCP, RAG, and the API. Build it once in `/lab` and check off all three.
- **Power-user Month 5 (evals)** is the skill to protect if time runs short. Apply it to whatever you built in `/lab`.
- **Course Month 5 (math)** comes last, once you have real systems to explain.
