# Month 5 Capstone — Theory Meets Practice

Use everything from Month 5 to revisit an earlier project with real rigor. The point
is to *close the loop*: the math you just learned should now improve work you already did.

## Pick one path
1. **Explain & improve a model** — take your Month 2 or 3 project. Add a proper
   statistical analysis of the data, justify your model choice with the math, and
   improve a metric. Document *why* the improvement worked in math terms.
2. **Build it from scratch** — re-implement one component you previously used as a
   black box (cosine-similarity search, linear regression, or gradient descent for a
   small net) using only NumPy. Show it matches the library.
3. **Full data-science study** — take a fresh dataset through the complete pipeline:
   EDA → statistics → modeling → an honest written conclusion with uncertainty.

## Requirements
- A clean notebook with narrative: each step explains the *why*, not just the *what*.
- At least one place where the **math from this month** directly informs a decision.
- Honest evaluation, including limitations and what you'd do next.
- A `README.md` summarizing the question, method, and finding.

## Done when
You can point at any piece of your AI stack — an embedding, a training step, a
prediction probability — and explain the mathematics underneath it from memory.
